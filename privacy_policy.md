# Privacy Policy for RedYoshiBot & CTGP-7 Network

**Last updated:** May 15th, 2026

## 1. Introduction

RedYoshiBot ("the Bot") is a Discord bot operated by the CTGP-7 community Discord server. Beyond standard Discord bot features (e.g., moderation, minigames, custom commands), the Bot also monitors and helps operate the **CTGP-7 Network**, the matchmaking and online play backend used by the CTGP-7 mod for Mario Kart 7. This policy explains what information the Bot and the CTGP-7 Network process, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

This policy supplements, and does not replace, [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms). Use of the Bot within Discord is also subject to Discord's Developer Policy.

## 2. Information Collected Through Discord

When the Bot is active in the server, or when you message it directly, it may process:

- **Discord identifiers**: your User ID, username, display name/nickname, and (for legacy accounts) discriminator.
- **Server roles and Nitro boost status**: used to determine permissions for certain commands and eligibility for beta access features (see Section 4).
- **Message content**: processed to recognize commands and other interactions with the Bot, perform automated moderation and safety checks (including detecting phishing or scam links), provide automated reminders and responses, and support community specific features and jokes. Direct messages sent to the Bot are also processed and forwarded to a private moderation log channel for moderation purposes.
- **Edited and deleted messages**: when a member edits or deletes a message, its content, author, channel, and timestamp are logged to a private moderation log channel.
- **Member join/leave events**: used for welcome/farewell messages, member count tracking, and to automatically remove any CTGP-7 console link (see Section 3) when you leave the server.

The following data may be created and stored along your Discord User ID by interacting with the Bot or the CTGP-7 Discord server:

- **"Facts"**: a short text snippet you may register, shown randomly to other users. You may register at most one, and can delete it yourself.
- **Bug reports**: issue descriptions you submit, along the report's open/closed status.
- **Moderation records**: warning counts and mute start times/durations.
- **Phishing/scam risk scores**: a temporary, in memory score tracked when your messages match known scam patterns (e.g., suspicious links combined with "free," "nitro," "gift," etc.). Reset automatically after a period of inactivity. On any score change, the message content that triggered the change and your Discord User ID are logged to a private moderation log channel.
- **Virtual score**: your "Yoshi Cookie" score, adjusted by playing the Bot's minigames. This score has no real world value.

## 3. Linking Your CTGP-7 Console to Your Discord Account

The CTGP-7 mod assigns each installation a **console ID** (a hardware derived identifier). You can optionally link this console ID to your Discord account using a one time code generated by the CTGP-7 plugin and the `@RedYoshiBot server link` command.

Once linked:

- Your Discord account and console ID are stored together as a linked pair.
- Your console is granted a "Discord Link" badge.
- Discord roles reflecting your console's status on the CTGP-7 Network (e.g., Player, Bronze, Silver, Gold) are automatically applied and periodically re-evaluated.
- The Bot may read your Discord roles and Nitro boost status to determine whether your linked console qualifies for beta access features.

You can remove this link at any time with `@RedYoshiBot server unlink`, which removes the "Discord Link" badge and associated roles. The link is also removed automatically if you leave the Discord server. Bot operators can additionally look up an existing link, transfer a console's data to a new console ID (e.g., if you get a new device), or fully clear ("prune") a console's stored data, including its link, ratings, points, badges, and status flags.

## 4. CTGP-7 Online Play Data

Independently of any Discord link, the CTGP-7 Network processes information generated by playing CTGP-7, whether or not your console is linked to a Discord account:

- **Console ID**: as described in Section 3.
- **In-game player name and "Mii name"**: the nickname set in-game, plus a timestamped history of names previously used by that console.
- **Mii icon**: the in-game Mii image associated with the console, which can be retrieved and displayed by the Bot operators.
- **Online ratings and statistics**: VR (rating) for Custom Track and Countdown online modes, Weekly Challenge points, race points, and aggregate gameplay statistics (races, time trials, battles, missions, and tracks played).
- **Verification, legality, and admin flags**: status markers used to identify verified players, flag consoles trying to spoof the **console ID**, or grant elevated permissions on the online service.
- **Badges and Achievements**: awarded automatically (e.g., for Weekly Challenge placements or completing certain goals) or manually.
- **Connection information necessary for online play**: information needed to host and join multiplayer rooms (matchmaking). This is used transiently to connect players and is not retained as part of long term profiles beyond the gameplay statistics above.

**Live multiplayer presence:** While you are participating in a CTGP-7 online room, the Bot posts and updates messages in designated Discord channels showing information about the room and its players, including in-game names, VR/rating, connection quality indicators, voice chat usage, and verification status. Information about public rooms is displayed in a public channel, while information about private rooms is displayed in a private moderation only channel. The Bot also records room logs, including participating console IDs and in-game names, in a private moderation log channel. Room status messages are removed when the room closes, but moderation logs are retained as part of the moderation log channel's history.

## 5. Aggregate / Statistical Information

The Bot displays community wide statistics that are not tied to individual identities, including total launches, races, missions, and battles played; the most played custom and original tracks; and daily counts of game launches and unique consoles (shown as historical usage graphs). These figures are derived from the data described in Section 4 but are presented in aggregate form.

## 6. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

- Operate Bot commands, minigames, and community features.
- Operate and moderate the CTGP-7 Network service (matchmaking, ratings, Weekly Challenges, badges, fair play enforcement).
- Enforce server and online play rules, including warnings, mutes, kicks, bans, and console status flags.
- Detect and respond to spam, phishing, and scam attempts.
- Display community and gameplay statistics (member counts, leaderboards, usage graphs, most played tracks).
- Grant Discord roles and badges reflecting a linked console's online play status, and beta access eligibility.

We do not use this information for advertising, or for any purpose unrelated to operating the Bot, the Discord server, and the CTGP-7 Network.

## 7. Data Storage and Retention

- Discord side records (warnings, mutes, facts, bug reports) are stored in a database operated alongside the Bot for as long as needed for the related feature, or until deleted as described in Section 9.
- CTGP-7 online data (console links, ratings, points, badges, name history, verification/legality flags) is stored in a separate database operated as part of the CTGP-7 Network services, retained until you unlink, request deletion or it is cleared by an operator.
- Daily aggregate statistics are retained to generate historical usage graphs and do not identify individual players.
- Live multiplayer room messages are removed once the corresponding room closes, private room logs persist in the moderation log channel.

## 8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

- We do not sell information or share it with advertisers or unrelated third parties.
- Live room information, leaderboards, badge lists, and registered facts are visible to other members of the Discord server (and, for public rooms, to anyone able to view the relevant channel), since these features are inherently community facing.
- Discord account links, Mii name history, Mii icons, and detailed moderation logs are accessible only within private channels used to operate the Bot and the online services.

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

- **Facts:** Delete your own registered fact at any time using the Bot's "delete fact" command.
- **Console link:** Unlink your console from your Discord account at any time with `@RedYoshiBot server unlink`. This also removes the associated badge and roles.
- **Console data** (ratings, points, badges, name history, legality status): this data exists independently of any Discord link and supports the integrity of online play (fair play enforcement, leaderboards, Weekly Challenges). You may request removal of data associated with your console ID. You may disable all communication with the Bot and online services from the in-game CTGP-7 server settings.
- **Discord side moderation records** (warnings, mutes): retained for the integrity of server moderation.
- **Leaving the server:** stops further Discord side data collection and automatically removes any console link, but previously recorded moderation history may persist. As described in Section 4, the CTGP-7 Network  will continue processing information generated by playing CTGP-7.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Bot or the CTGP-7 online services. Material changes will be announced in the server's announcements channel. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
